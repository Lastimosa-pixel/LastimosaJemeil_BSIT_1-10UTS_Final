Social Self

Socially, I’m not the most outgoing person. I tend to keep my circle small. I value real connections, not just surface-level conversations. Being around people can be draining sometimes, but when I find those who understand me, I open up more. I think my role in social groups is usually the quiet observer—the one who listens more than talks. Still, I try to be kind and supportive to those around me.
