Introduction (About Me)

My name is Jemeil Lastimosa, and I am a first-year BSIT student from section 1-10. I’m someone who enjoys simple things—like drawing and going outside to clear my mind. These hobbies help me feel calm and stay connected with myself.

My journey hasn’t always been easy. I've had highs and lows, but even when I'm down, I try to stay grounded and focused. Life has its struggles, but I always find a way to rise above them. One thing that has helped me is being kind—not just to others, but also to myself.

I see myself as a quiet but strong person. I work best when I’m on my own, and that’s where I feel most in control. My biggest strength is doing things alone. At the same time, I know I need to improve in opening up and asking for help when needed.

For this project, I chose to work with HTML and CSS. These tools reflect my creativity and the technical side of me. I already have some experience using them, so they feel natural to me. They also show how I like building things from scratch, organizing them in my own style, and paying attention to details.

Conclusion

This course and project helped me reflect more on who I am. I learned that “The Self” is more than just one part of me—it’s a mix of how I think, feel, and act in different areas of life.
